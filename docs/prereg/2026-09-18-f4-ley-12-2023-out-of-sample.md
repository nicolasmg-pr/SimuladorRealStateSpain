## Predicted direction, before running

**The in-regime span, quoted from §7.2's F4 text**, is bounded by two independent readings of the
statute in force:

| source | tenancies / contracts | rent |
|---|---|---|
| register [O-HB no. 4] | **+1,374** contracts | **−2.7%** real, new contracts |
| Pérez García | **−13%** tenancies | effect **≈0** |

So the span admits a rent effect from ≈0 to −2.7%, and a contract effect from clearly positive to
−13%. **What it does not admit is a rise in rents.**

| Quantity | Predicted sign | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| tensioned contract rent | **negative or ≈0**, and above all **not a rise** | The cap binds on the grandes tenedores' units only, so the direct bite is small; the previous-contract + IRAV anchor holds the rest of the stock near its own lagged rent rather than releasing it upward. §5b.3 fixed the reference index to the discount it declares, so the bite lands on the upper tail rather than on every unit |
| new leases, tensioned | **negative, and no larger than −13%** | The arbitrage condition `cap < r_req` fires for the minority of units the index actually binds; the majority face no index at all, so the withdrawal margin has far less mass to act on than under Ley 11/2020 |

**F4 fires if tensioned contract rent RISES.** That is the failure condition §7.2 wrote for
itself, and it is the sign the model produced under this statute the last time it was read
(+16.6%, `rent-cap-state-law`, pre-§7.2b and pre-§5b.3, never re-measured).

A second, weaker way to fail: the sign is right but the contraction in leases is far outside
−13%. That is a magnitude failure, recorded as one, and it does **not** spend the evidence the
way an inverted sign does — §7.2's F4 is written about the rise, not the size.

## Reporting category claimed

**Direction, and only direction**, per `model-spec §13.1`/§13.2 and §7.2's own "Reporting
consequence": if F1–F3 pass and F4 lands inside the span, Ley 12/2023 moves from **not
reportable** to **direction** — *not* to magnitude, which the variance rule refuses while the
structural parameters behind the exit margin remain guesses. No number produced by this run is
citable as a quantity, whichever way it falls.

If F4 lands **outside** the span, the regime stays **not reportable** and the result is registered
as a falsification of §7.2's arbitrage condition. It is not repaired by re-fitting, and any repair
attempted afterwards is recorded as having spent Ley 12/2023 as validating evidence.

## Result (filled in after the run)

- **Run artefacts:** `runs/f4-ley12-2023-seeds1-10-a5db64c429.csv` (per-seed, ten seeds), produced
  by `_rent_cap_response_per_seed(range(1, 11), index_binds_all=False)` — the same helper every
  Ley 11/2020 figure in `tests/test_validation.py` is measured with, imported rather than
  re-implemented so the two regimes are comparable by construction.
- **Measured:**

| | pooled | sd | se | t | seeds negative | min | max |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| tensioned contract rent | **−0.79%** | 5.03pp | 1.59pp | −0.50 | 6/10 | −9.13% | +7.25% |
| new leases, tensioned | **−8.06%** | 7.10pp | 2.24pp | −3.59 | 8/10 | −20.02% | +0.56% |

- **Prediction held / failed: HELD. F4 does not fire.**

  **The failure condition does not occur.** §7.2 wrote F4 about a **rise** in tensioned contract
  rent, and there is no rise: the pooled reading is −0.79%. The +16.6% this statute produced the
  last time it was read (`rent-cap-state-law`, pre-§7.2b and pre-§5b.3) is gone.

  **Both legs land inside the pre-registered span.** Rent −0.79% against a span of ≈0 to −2.7%;
  leases −8.06% against a span of clearly positive to −13%.

  **And the rent leg lands inside it by sitting on the span's zero end, not by measuring a fall.**
  |mean| < 1 se, t = −0.50, with four of the ten seeds positive: the rent effect under this
  statute is **statistically indistinguishable from zero**. That is Pérez García's endpoint
  ("rent effect ≈0") reproduced, and it is the honest reading — the model does not measure a
  −2.7% fall here, it measures nothing distinguishable from no effect. By the ten-seed sign
  standard §7.2b's G1 applies to Ley 11/2020 (correct sign at *every* seed), this rent leg would
  not clear. F4's text does not ask that of it, and this record does not pretend it did.

  **The lease leg is the one that carries a sign.** −8.06%, t = −3.59, negative at eight of ten
  seeds, inside the span and nowhere near its −13% edge.

  **Not reported: the co-movement ratio.** Δln leases / Δln rent computes to 10.6 here, and that
  number is an artefact of dividing by a denominator indistinguishable from zero, not a
  statistic. It is recorded only so that nobody recomputes it and reports it.

- **Reporting category, per what was claimed above:** Ley 12/2023 moves from **not reportable** to
  **direction**, and only on the **quantity** leg — the cap contracts new leases in the tensioned
  zone. The rent leg does not acquire a direction, because the measurement does not support one;
  what it acquires is the absence of the rise, which is exactly what F4 was written to test. **No
  quantity from this run is citable as a magnitude**, as pre-registered, whichever way it fell.

- **The evidence is not spent.** §7.2 was not rewritten in response to this run, because it did not
  fail. Ley 12/2023 remains available as validating evidence — and this file is the record that
  the one shot was taken on 2026-09-18 at commit `21a5623`, against a span fixed in writing
  beforehand at commit `4c4eb58`.
